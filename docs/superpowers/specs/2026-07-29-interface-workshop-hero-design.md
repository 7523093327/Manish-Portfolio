# Interface Workshop Hero Design

## Purpose

Position Manish Gaud as a freelance multidisciplinary product designer for SaaS, fintech, and AI-workflow teams. The hero must communicate the design process at a glance and lead visitors to view selected work or start a project.

## Visual direction

Warm off-white paper ground with subtle grain. The central asset is an original, hand-drawn horizontal process illustration: messy product inputs on the left flow through mapping and component-shaping stations to a polished dashboard on the right. A lightweight, ink-drawn conveyor belt visually connects every stage. Dark ink outlines are accented with cobalt blue, tangerine orange, and lime.

## Content

- Navigation: Work, Services, Tools & Tech Stack, About, Testimonials, Contact; primary action: Start a Project.
- Eyebrow: `Product Designer · SaaS · Fintech · AI Workflow`
- Heading: `I shape messy product ideas into interfaces people understand.`
- Supporting copy: `I help SaaS, fintech, and growing teams simplify workflows, redesign dashboards, and build polished digital experiences.`
- Primary CTA: View Selected Work
- Secondary CTA: Start a Project
- Trust line: `Worked across Zacks Investment Research, PeopleHum, Cosoot, and Zenskar.`

## Motion and accessibility

On page load, the left-side input cards enter first, the process stations illuminate in sequence, and the final UI is revealed last. Hovering the illustration adds restrained arrow drift, a light sticky-note wiggle, and a soft UI glow. All movement stops or resolves to its end state when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is active. The layout must scale cleanly from desktop to tablet and mobile.
